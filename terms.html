Website Partnership Terms

These terms explain what you receive, what you agree to provide, and how the website exchange works. Please read them before the project begins.

Applies to the ₦0/$0 business website offer

Partnership Terms Privacy Policy

1.The Agreement

You will receive a professional business website without paying a website design or development fee. In exchange, you agree to provide an honest video testimonial after the website has been completed and approved.

2.Your Website

Your website includes:

  • Up to 4 standard pages
  • A design created around your business
  • Mobile-friendly design
  • 1 round of changes
  • Standard website setup and launch

The exact pages and content will be agreed before work begins.

3.What You Provide

You agree to provide the information needed to build your website. This may include business information, logo, images, services or product information, contact information, and website wording/content.

You confirm that you have permission to use any images, logos, text or other materials you provide. You also agree to provide feedback and requested information within a reasonable amount of time so the project can be completed.

4.Content

You are responsible for providing the main information that needs to appear on your website. Small improvements or adjustments to your existing wording may be made where appropriate. Writing large amounts of new content from scratch is not included unless separately agreed.

5.Changes

You receive 1 round of changes. After seeing the first completed version, please send all the changes you would like together. Extra rounds of changes or major changes to the original project may be charged separately. Nothing extra will be charged without discussing it with you first.

6.When the Website Is Complete

You'll be given the opportunity to review your website. You can either approve it or send your included changes. Once those changes have been completed and you approve the website, the website is considered finished.

7.Your Video Testimonial

After approving the website, you agree to record and send an honest video testimonial within 7 calendar days. The video can be recorded using your phone and does not need professional equipment.

You are not required to say anything you don't believe or give a positive review. We simply ask that you honestly describe your experience and thoughts about the completed website.

8.Using Your Testimonial

By submitting your testimonial, you give permission for the video to be used to show others examples of previous work and customer experiences. This can include use on websites, social media, portfolio, case studies, proposals, presentations, and promotional materials and advertisements.

Parts of the video may be shortened, captioned or reformatted for different platforms. Your words will not intentionally be edited in a way that changes what you meant. You also give permission for your name, business name and publicly available business branding to appear alongside the testimonial where appropriate.

9.Showing the Website

You give permission for the completed website and screenshots of it to be shown as an example of completed work — including in portfolios, social media, case studies, proposals and other promotional materials. Private information that isn't publicly displayed on your website will not intentionally be shared.

10.The 7-Day Requirement

Your testimonial must be submitted within 7 calendar days after you approve your completed website. The complimentary website is being provided as part of this exchange — until your part of the exchange has been completed, final handover of the website is not required.

If the agreed testimonial isn't received within the 7 days, the website may be taken offline and the complimentary project may be closed.

11.Website Address and Hosting

Standard website setup can be provided without a monthly hosting charge under the normal setup used for this offer. You are responsible for purchasing and renewing your own domain name if you want a custom website address.

If you prefer another hosting company or request something that requires a paid service, you will be responsible for that cost — any paid cost will be discussed before it is incurred. Third-party services can change their prices, rules or availability in the future, so free hosting cannot be guaranteed forever.

12.What Is Not Included

Unless specifically agreed, this offer does not include: more than 4 pages, online stores, advanced booking systems, CRM systems, business automations, third-party integrations, custom software, ongoing website updates, ongoing website management, paid tools or services, major copywriting projects, or SEO campaigns. These can be discussed separately if your business needs them.

13.Delays

The project depends on you providing the requested information, content and feedback. If information or feedback isn't provided for a long period, the project may be paused or closed.

14.Business Results

Your website will be built to professionally represent your business based on the agreed project. However, no specific number of customers, enquiries, website visitors, Google rankings, sales or revenue can be guaranteed.

15.Ending the Project

Either side can choose to end the project before it is completed. If the project ends before the agreed exchange is completed, unfinished designs, website files or other work do not automatically have to be handed over.

These terms are confirmed together as part of your project agreement once your business is accepted — you don't need to sign anything to express interest. If anything here is unclear, ask about it before getting started.
