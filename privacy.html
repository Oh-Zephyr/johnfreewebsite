Privacy Policy

What information is collected when you reach out about the ₦0/$0 website offer, and how it's used.

Applies to johnfreewebsite.vercel.app

Partnership Terms Privacy Policy

1.What This Covers

This page explains what happens with your information when you visit this site or reach out about the business website offer. It doesn't cover WhatsApp, Instagram or any other platform's own handling of your data — those are covered by their own privacy policies.

2.What Gets Collected

This site itself doesn't have a contact form, account system or tracking script — it doesn't collect anything automatically just by being visited.

Information is only collected when you choose to reach out, and only what you share directly:

  • Whatever you send via WhatsApp — your phone number, name and message
  • Business information, logo, images and content you provide if your business is accepted
  • Your testimonial video and any details you share in it

3.How It's Used

Information you share is used to: work out whether your business is a good fit for the offer, build and launch your website, and stay in touch about the project. It isn't sold, and it isn't shared with anyone outside of what's needed to build and host your website.

4.Testimonials & Website Examples

If your business is accepted, your testimonial video and completed website may be used as examples of past work, as described in the Website Partnership Terms. That's the only case where information is used publicly.

5.Cookies & Tracking

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Photography is loaded from Unsplash's content delivery network, which may log standard technical request data (like any image host does) — that's outside this site's control.

6.Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel. Standard technical logs (like the kind any web host keeps) may be generated as part of normal hosting — this isn't used for tracking or marketing.

7.How Long Information Is Kept

Information shared while discussing or building your website is kept for as long as it's needed for the project, plus a reasonable period afterward for the agreed use of your testimonial and website examples described in the Partnership Terms.

8.Your Choices

You can ask what information is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted where it's no longer needed for an active project or an agreed use — just reach out on WhatsApp or Instagram.

9.Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time as the site or offer changes. The version here always reflects current practice.

Questions about your information? Reach out the same way you would to get started — WhatsApp or Instagram — and it'll be answered directly.
